# Product Launch Strategy

## Task Description
Develop a comprehensive strategy for launching our new product line targeting the millennial demographic.

## Requirements
- Market research on target audience preferences and buying behaviors
- Competitive analysis of similar product launches
- Pricing strategy considering market positioning
- Marketing channel selection (social media, influencers, traditional media)
- Timeline for pre-launch, launch, and post-launch activities
- Budget allocation across different marketing channels
- Success metrics and KPIs to measure launch effectiveness
- Risk assessment and mitigation strategies

## Priority
High - Product launch date is approaching

## Expected Outcome
Complete product launch strategy document with actionable steps, timelines, and success metrics.

## Deadline
2 weeks from now
