# Customer Retention Strategy

## Task Description
Develop a comprehensive customer retention strategy to reduce churn rate and increase customer lifetime value.

## Requirements
- Analyze current customer churn data and identify patterns
- Segment customers based on retention risk levels
- Identify key factors affecting customer satisfaction
- Research best practices for customer retention
- Design loyalty program framework
- Create personalized retention campaigns
- Establish customer feedback collection system
- Define success metrics and monitoring process

## Priority
High - Churn rate has increased 15% in last quarter

## Expected Outcome
Complete customer retention strategy with actionable initiatives and measurable goals.

## Deadline
End of this month
