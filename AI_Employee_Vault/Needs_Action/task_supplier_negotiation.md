# Supplier Contract Negotiation

## Task Description
Renegotiate contracts with key suppliers to reduce costs and improve terms for the upcoming fiscal year.

## Requirements
- Review current supplier contracts and identify renewal dates
- Analyze spending patterns and volume commitments
- Research alternative suppliers for leverage in negotiations
- Prepare negotiation strategy and target pricing
- Identify areas for improvement (delivery, quality, terms)
- Schedule meetings with key suppliers
- Document agreed terms and updated contracts
- Update procurement database with new terms

## Priority
Medium - Contracts expire in 3 months

## Expected Outcome
Updated supplier contracts with improved terms and cost savings achieved.

## Deadline
End of next month
